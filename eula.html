End-user license agreement

Effective 18 November 2026. This agreement covers Mason Levey QuickBooks Bridge ("the Bridge"), a private tool operated by Mason Levey LLC ("the operator") for the companies whose books it keeps.

Who may use the Bridge

Only Mason Levey LLC and the people each connected company authorises to keep its books may use the Bridge, and only for that company's own bookkeeping and reporting.

What the Bridge does

With the connected company's permission, given through Intuit's QuickBooks Online sign-in, the Bridge reads the connected company's accounting data and creates, updates or voids accounting records when an authorised user asks it to. It never deletes records.

Your responsibilities

Authorised users check each change before asking the Bridge to make it, keep their sign-in details private, and tell the operator about any suspected misuse.

Ending access

A connected company can disconnect the Bridge at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings, Apps). The operator can also stop the Bridge at any time.
