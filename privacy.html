Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026. This policy explains how Mason Levey QuickBooks Bridge ("the Bridge"), operated by Mason Levey LLC for itself and the clients whose books it keeps, handles data from QuickBooks Online.

What data the Bridge accesses

Only the QuickBooks Online accounting data of the companies that connect it: reports, accounts, vendors, customers, bills, invoices, payments, journal entries and similar records. The Bridge does not request access to payroll or payment processing.

How it is used

Solely for the bookkeeping, financial reports and analysis Mason Levey LLC provides to that company. The data is not sold, rented, shared for advertising, or used for any other company.

Where it is kept

The Bridge runs on the operator's own computer. Connection keys are stored encrypted. Request logs and a log of each change made to QuickBooks are kept on that computer; request logs are deleted automatically after a short period.

Who can see it

The operator and the people each company authorises to keep its books. No other party receives a client's QuickBooks data through the Bridge.

Your choices

A connected company can disconnect the Bridge at any time in QuickBooks Online (Settings, Apps). After disconnection the Bridge can no longer read or change that company's data, and the company can ask the operator to delete the logs it holds.
